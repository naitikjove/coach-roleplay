Chapter: **How Can You Lead with Emotional Intelligence?**. Each beat tests exactly one video and one competency.

Note: the practice (Theo) and certificate (Jenna) roleplays use the same two videos on purpose; the stories are different.

## What each beat tests
- **Beat A (41989):** Jenna, angry that a teammate's forecast went to the director with wrong numbers, wants to email the whole team a list of his mistakes. Learner stops it, finds out what went wrong, tells Jenna to own how she spoke and her part, and sets a private talk with Ryan at a time.
- **Beat B (41995):** Kayla asked to move a client deadline and gave no reason. Jenna wants to just say yes. Learner says what Jenna has noticed, asks what is going on and keeps Friday for the client.
