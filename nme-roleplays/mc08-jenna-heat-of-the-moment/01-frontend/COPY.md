## Title
Heat of the Moment

## Scenario (brief card)
You lead a small team of analysts. This morning, Ryan's monthly sales forecast went to the director with wrong numbers again. In the team meeting, Jenna, a senior analyst, told Ryan his work was sloppy, in front of everyone. She is still angry and has written an email to the whole team listing his mistakes, which she wants to send before lunch. Jenna also wants your advice on Kayla, another teammate, who asked to move a client deadline and did not say why. Help Jenna decide what to do about both.

## About
- Who: Jenna is a senior analyst on your team. Ryan and Kayla are her teammates.
- What: Ryan's monthly sales forecast went to the director with wrong numbers. Jenna told him off in front of the team.
- Why now: Jenna wants to email the whole team about Ryan's mistakes before lunch. Kayla has also asked to move a client deadline.

## Your Objectives
1. Jenna wants to email the whole team about Ryan's wrong forecast before lunch. Decide with her what to do about the email.
2. Kayla, a teammate, asked to move a client deadline and did not say why. Agree with Jenna how she'll answer Kayla.

## Who is in the Conversation

**You — Team lead**  
You lead a small team of analysts. Jenna, Ryan and Kayla all report to you.

**Jenna — Senior analyst**  
Sharp and hard-working. She wants the numbers right, and she is angry that they weren't.

## Entry card body
Show how you'd handle an angry teammate and a teammate who may be struggling.
