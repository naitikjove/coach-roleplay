## Title
When a Team Member Leaves

## Scenario (brief card)
You manage a team of five people. Lucas, your most experienced team member, is moving to lead a new team in three weeks. He does two jobs that no one else does. Each week, he decides who works on what. He also checks the team's work before clients see it. Clients call him directly whenever they have a question. Your manager, Rebecca, wants your team to be ready without him. She has approved hiring one new person and set up a meeting to plan the next three weeks. Share your plan with her.

## Your Objectives
1. Lucas is the only one who plans the team's week and checks work before clients see it. Agree with Rebecca who will do these jobs after he moves.
2. Clients go to Lucas with every question. Agree with Rebecca who they should go to now.
3. You're interviewing two people for the new role this week. Tell Rebecca how you'll run the interviews and what you'll ask.

## Who is in the Conversation

**You — Manager**  
You manage a team of five people, including Lucas.

**Rebecca — Your manager**  
She manages you and approved hiring one new person.
