## Title
Coaching Hannah on the New Hires

## Scenario (brief card)
You manage the client teams at a small marketing agency. Hannah leads the account team. Six new people joined her team this year. Two have already left, and two more look unhappy. Hannah plans to book a two-day course for the new hires, and the invoice would come to you. She asked to talk to you before she does. Help her work out what to do.

## About lines
- Who: Hannah leads your account team. Joel leads another team. Both report to you.
- What: Six new people joined Hannah’s team this year. Two have left, and two more look unhappy. Hannah plans to book a two-day course for them.
- Why now: Hannah asked to talk to you before the course invoice arrives.

## Your Objectives
1. Hannah plans to book a two-day course for the new hires. Decide with her what to do.
2. Two of the six new hires have left, and two more look unhappy. Agree with Hannah what, if anything, should change for new hires.
3. All four new hires on Joel’s team are still there. Decide with Hannah what to do about that.

## Who is in the Conversation

**You — Manager**  
You manage the client teams at a small marketing agency. Hannah and Joel each lead a team that reports to you.

**Hannah — Leads the account team**  
Warm and a bit rushed. Proud of her team, and worried that new people keep leaving.
