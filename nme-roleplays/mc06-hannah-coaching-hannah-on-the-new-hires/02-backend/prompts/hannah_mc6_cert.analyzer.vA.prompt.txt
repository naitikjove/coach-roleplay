Definitions:

Examples: give each new hire a first real task; tell them on day one what to work on in week one; check in with them at the end of day one; walk them through the first week; set a plan for the first week.   - Reason: why it helps. Examples: so they know what to do; so they stay; so they don't sit around waiting; so they feel useful from the start; so they get going faster; so they don't leave.
Range 8-10: The change AND the reason.

A check-in with each new hire at the end of day one is a CHANGE (it counts in the 8-10 and 5-7 ranges), not this follow-up. If the learner gave one, mention it in the note as a strength.   - The learner had NO earlier Joel help and now says no, says Joel is busy, tells her to just use the checklist, asks about Joel and then says no, or answers with anything that is not a yes = C 0 (range "0-1", check "fixed"). The learner turned down the right move.
  - The learner had earlier named Joel's help (for example "ask Joel for tips") and now says no to the walk-through but keeps Joel helping in some way = no change to the range. They still brought Joel in.
