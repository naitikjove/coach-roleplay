## Title
Performance and Feedback in Practice

## Scenario (brief card)
Last week your events team of five ran the company's customer conference, and it's over. Zara led the sponsor program, Felix looked after the sponsor banners, and Ravi, the newest on the team, handles vendor invoices. Your manager, Rebecca, presents the conference to leadership in about ten minutes. Before she goes in, she wants to go through a few things from the week with you, and she'll ask what you plan to do about each one.

## About lines
- You manage the events team. Zara, Felix and Ravi report to you.
- Your team ran the customer conference last week.
- Rebecca, your manager, has about ten minutes before she presents it to leadership and wants to go through a few things first.

## Your Objectives
1. Zara ran the sponsor program well but did almost everything herself. Tell Rebecca what you'll say in her review.
2. Leadership will ask if the conference was worth the investment. Tell Rebecca what you'll put at the top of the team debrief.
3. Felix changed an approved sponsor banner and told no one, so the logo was cut off. Tell Rebecca what you'll say to him.
4. Ravi, new on your team, had three invoices go to the wrong person, so three vendors were paid late. Tell Rebecca what you'll do.

## Who is in the Conversation

**You — Team manager**  
You manage the events team. Zara, Felix and Ravi report to you.

**Rebecca — Your manager**  
Your manager. Direct and short on time.

## Scene intro (app)
Same as the scenario above (the app reuses the scenario text as the scene intro for this roleplay).

## How it works (app)
Rebecca starts the talk. Answer her out loud, like a real one-on-one. When you’re done, end the conversation to get your feedback.

## Entry purpose line (app)
Show you can manage team performance and feedback. The conference is over, and Rebecca wants to go through a few things from the week before she presents to leadership.
