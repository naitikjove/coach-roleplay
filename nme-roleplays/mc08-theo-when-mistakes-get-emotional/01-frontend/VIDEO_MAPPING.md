Chapter: **How Can You Lead with Emotional Intelligence?**. Each beat tests exactly one video and one competency.

Note: the practice (Theo) and certificate (Jenna) roleplays use the same two videos on purpose; the stories are different.

## What each beat tests
- **Beat A (41989):** Theo wants to send an excuse reply to a client who complained about a late report. Learner stops it, finds out what happened, tells Theo to own the miss and gives Mark a next date.
- **Beat B (41995):** Theo wants to tell Devon (whose numbers keep arriving late) straight away that it is not good enough. Learner says what Theo has noticed, asks what is going on and keeps the Monday-evening deadline.
