## Title
The Delegation Dilemma

## Scenario (brief card)
Amara plans the weekly work for your team of six. A new request just came in from the sales team: a short comparison of how two competitors price their service, due at the end of the month. Dev has been on your team the longest and prepares the monthly customer report. Lena joined three months ago. Amara asked to talk with you about who should take the request, how to keep track of it, and how to recognize something Dev did last month.

## About (summary lines)
- Who: Amara plans the work for your team of six. Dev is your longest-serving team member, and he prepares the monthly report your team sends to customers. Lena joined three months ago.
- What: A new request from the sales team is due in about three weeks.
- Why now: Amara needs your decision on the request. She also wants to talk about Dev’s work on last month’s customer report.

## Your Objectives
1. Amara leans toward Dev for the sales team’s request. Decide with her who should take it.
2. Amara wants to follow the work closely. Agree with her how to keep track of it.
3. Dev stayed late to fix mistakes in last month’s customer report. Decide with Amara how to thank him.

## Who is in the Conversation

**You — Team lead**  
Dev, Lena and Amara are three of the six people on your team.

**Amara — Plans the weekly work**  
Careful and organized. Brings you her ideas before she acts.

## Intro and how it works
- Intro: Amara asked to talk with you about a new request from the sales team and about Dev’s work last month.
- How it works: Amara starts the talk. Answer out loud, like a real conversation. When you’re done, end the conversation to get your feedback.
- Format note: Microphone required · CTA: Join Conversation
