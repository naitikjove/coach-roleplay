# Locked learner-facing copy (staging-aligned)

## Title
Handling a client situation

## Scenario (brief card)
You lead a team of five, all working on a big delivery due in a week for Brightway, an important client. Dana, your most experienced team member, is in charge. By your plan from three weeks ago, the work should be 75% done, but it's only 50%, even with extra hours. To make things harder, Brightway now wants one more thing added to the same delivery. Dana is worried and wants to talk. As her manager, help her resolve the situation.

## Your Objectives
1. Your team is behind, and Dana wants everyone to work this weekend to catch up. Decide with her how to get back on track.
2. Dana wants you to talk to Jake because he missed a request from Brightway. Decide what you'll do.
3. Brightway wants a new reporting dashboard, and Dana wants to promise it by the same due date. Agree with her how to handle it.
4. Dana asks who should take on the dashboard. Agree with her how you'll choose.

## Who is in the Conversation

**You — Manager**  
You lead a team of five, including Dana.

**Dana — Direct report**  
Your most experienced team member. In charge of the Brightway delivery.

## Entry card body
Help Dana get a late client delivery back on track and handle a new request from the client.
