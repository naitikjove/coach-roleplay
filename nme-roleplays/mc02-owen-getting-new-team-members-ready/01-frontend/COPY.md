## Title
Getting New Team Members Ready

## Scenario (brief card)
You are Owen's manager. Until last month, you and Owen did the same job, side by side. Owen helps new people get started on your team. He gets each one a laptop and logins, and gives them a buddy: an experienced teammate who shows them the work. You set a goal: by the end of week two, new people should do their own work without help. This month, three people joined. Only one reached the goal. You asked to meet with Owen. Help him get things back on track.

## Your Objectives
1. Owen thinks two of the new people just didn't try hard enough. Decide with him what to do about the missed goal.
2. Owen wants you to complain to the head of IT about late laptops. Decide how you'll respond.
3. Six new people are joining next month, not two. Agree with Owen how to handle this with the team.
4. Your team can't agree on the best way to teach new people. Agree with Owen how that choice gets made.

## Who is in the Conversation

**You — Manager**  
You manage Owen. Until last month, you did the same job, side by side.

**Owen — Former peer, now reports to you**  
Helps new people get started on your team: a laptop, logins, and a buddy for each one.

## Entry card body
Show you can think like a manager. Owen helps new people get started, and only one of three reached the goal this month. He used to be your peer. Help him get things back on track.
