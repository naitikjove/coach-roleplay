COMPETENCY RUBRICS (score 1–10 when observed)

### building_trust — Building Trust
Core job: take Jordan’s reality seriously while leading.
Strong signals: interest in how work runs; invite/reflect; learn-before-big-redesign; small concrete follow-through; honest humility.
Weak signals: pure process monologue with zero interest; mockery; empty slogans only.

### expectation_setting — Expectation Setting
Core job: under peer-habit pressure, don’t fully return to pure peer labor; don’t destroy the relationship with rank-only flex.
Strong signals: work of the favor stays with Jordan; calm firmness; optional non-takeover help; how asks/priorities work next time.
Weak signals: full IC takeover as the plan; harsh shame; endless soft maybe with no ownership line.
not_observed: no peer-habit/favor/takeover pressure appears.

### delegation — Delegation
Core job: direct work with ownership + clarity; don’t reclaim as hero IC; don’t pure-dump.
Strong signals: Jordan owns the larger work; rough outcome/timing/done-looks-like; support or sensible check-in (not hover).
Weak signals: learner keeps the project as IC; zero ownership/clarity; micromanage every step.
not_observed: no larger ownership/project discussion appears.

### accountability — Accountability
Core job: under heat of a miss, own as manager, drive recovery, no scapegoat.
Strong signals: “on me / my team”; fix + rough timing; who talks to leadership; include Jordan in recovery.
Weak signals: throw Jordan under the bus; hide; comfort only with no ownership/plan.
not_observed: no miss/error/heat about outcomes appears.

Score anchors (when observed):
• 9–10: clear strong signals, little collapse
• 7–8: solid core job with minor gaps
• 5–6: mixed / partial
• 3–4: mostly weak signals
• 1–2: active collapse (scapegoat, full takeover as habit, pure steamroll)
