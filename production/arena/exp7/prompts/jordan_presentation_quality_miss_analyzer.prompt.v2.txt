PRESSURE A - building_trust
======================================================================
RIGHT patterns: asks what happened or why before prescribing; stays with her answer; acknowledges how it landed.
WRONG patterns: jumps straight to fixes/process/blame; steamrolls or shuts her down; treats it as pure logistics.

--- G1 (max 4) Listen / ask before redesigning ---
4 points: Learner asked what happened OR why AND stayed with her account after her first surface answer BEFORE any fix/process/deadline line.
  Example: "What happened with the deck?" then she explains, then learner responds without immediately assigning work.

3 points: Learner asked a real what/why once before fixing, but did not stay after her first surface answer OR mixed fix in same turn as the ask.
  Example: "Why did this happen?" then quickly "you should fix the data first."

1 point: Thin prompt only ("tell me more", "go on") then jumped to solutions with no real inquiry.
  Example: "Quickly tell me" then immediate rework instructions.

0 points: Went straight to solutions, process, blame, or deadlines without inviting her account.
  Example: "First work on the data" as first move on the rejection with no ask.

--- G2 (max 3) Rejection real for Claire, not only a task ---
3 points: Acknowledged how it landed on her AND reached or invited root cause (cause may be in same or later line).
  Example: "That must be frustrating" plus "what broke down in the story?"

2 points: Acknowledged her experience or feelings but never moved toward cause.
  Example: "I know this is hard" but only task talk after.

0 points: Pure work item; no human acknowledgment.
  Example: Only "fix the numbers" with no recognition of impact on her.

--- G3 (max 3) Does not steamroll ---
3 points: No process dump, shame, mockery, or shutdown before hearing her; learner left room for her to speak.
  Absence band: if administered and no steamroll/shutdown language, score 3 with empty quote (same every run).

1 point: Some steamrolling or edge ("come to the point", impatience) but she could still speak.
  Example: "Come to the point Claire" then still let her explain.

0 points: Process monologue, blame-dump, or talk-over as the main move with no interest in her account.

PRESSURE B - expectation_setting
======================================================================
RIGHT patterns: blocks send straight to leadership; says to me first / not leadership; names order and deadline.
WRONG patterns: Proceed/OK/yeah to send-up; no block; leaves path and timing to Claire.

--- G1 (max 4) Block Claire's leadership bypass ---
4 points: Clear block on send-up AND held when Claire pushed (or NO-PUSH if she never pushed after block).
  Examples (4): "No, send it to me first" and held; "Not to leadership" and held.

3 points: Clear block first, then only bare-acked her push (ok/okej) without re-blocking in words.
  Example: "No, send to me first" then Claire pushes timing worry, learner says only "ok".

1 point: Weak or hedged resistance only; no clear block.
  Example: "Are you sure?" or vague worry without blocking send-up.

0 points: Approved bypass, bare ack only to send-up plan, or no goal.
  Examples (0): Claire proposes send to leadership, learner "Proceed." or "OK" or "yeah" only; silence.

--- G2 (max 3) What comes first ---
3 points: Named what gets fixed first as its own step (fix content), OR required manager review in a distinct clause beyond the block line.
  Example: "Fix the data first, then send me the draft before leadership sees it."

2 points: Clear to-me-first / not-leadership that only implies review order — including the same block line already used for G1.
  Example: "Just send it to me" / "No, send it to me first" / "first send it to me for review" (review order implied). Max 2 when this is the only priority evidence.

1 point: Gestured at ordering without what comes first.
  Example: "We need to be careful about order" with no step named.

0 points: No priority; left whole recovery path to Claire.
  Example: Only reacted to send-up with emotion, never stated order.

HARD: Do not award G2 = 3 from the identical substring already scored as G1 block.

--- G3 (max 3) Real time ---
3 points: Specific handoff or deadline before leadership sees it again — must quote a Learner day/checkpoint.
  Example: "Send me a draft tomorrow"; "by Thursday before leadership."

1 point: Loose this-week-only framing in a Learner quote.
  Example: "Get it done this week" with no day or checkpoint.

0 points: Vague or no timing — including when timing only appears in Claire's lines.
  Example: No Learner time at all after bypass discussion. Empty quote ⇒ 0 (not an absence band).

PRESSURE C - delegation
======================================================================
RIGHT patterns: Claire owns rewrite; learner declines co-write/jump-in; offers review/check-in without taking draft.
WRONG patterns: "let's work on it together"; takes draft; exit without decline on pull.

--- G1 (max 4) Leaves rewrite with Claire ---
4 points: Clear Claire does the rewrite AND held when she pulled again (NO-PUSH if no second pull).
  Example: "You rewrite it and send me the draft" and held when she asked to co-write.

2 points: Clear decline of co-write / peer pull (including contextual no); OR assign-to-Claire + handoff before B send-up.
  Examples (2): "No" to co-write pull; "Fix this and give it to me" before send-up trap (Claire fixes, brings to manager).

0 points: Took draft, agreed to rewrite together, discuss-it-now joint rescue, or exit-only with no decline.
  Examples (0): "Yeah we can discuss it right now" as joint work; "bye" as only reply to pull; agreed to co-build.

--- G2 (max 3) Stays manager when peer habit shows ---
3 points: Redirected pull calmly AND Claire ownership explicit in learner's words.
  Example: "You're responsible for the rewrite; I'll review what you send."

1 point: Declined without ownership explicit, or wobbled.
  Example: Lone "no" to pull with no who-owns-it line (if that no not already used for G1).

0 points: Peer rescue mode or shame-only refusal.
  Example: Jumped in to fix slides together; only insulted her for asking.

Do not score G2 from same lone "no" already used for G1 unless second clause makes ownership explicit.

--- G3 (max 3) Help without taking draft ---
3 points: Real non-takeover support named.
  Example: "Send me your draft and I'll review"; "I'll unblock the data source."

1 point: Vague availability only.
  Example: "Let me know if you need anything."

0 points: No support, or support becomes doing the work.
  Example: "I'll help you rewrite the narrative" (takeover).

PRESSURE D - accountability
======================================================================
RIGHT patterns: names miss without scapegoating; Claire leads and answers in the leadership conversation; draft to manager before resubmit. Manager presence in the room is OK if Claire still owns Q&A.
WRONG patterns: takes leadership Q&A / answers for Claire; co-presents as joint defense; comfort only with no ownership line.

--- G1 (max 3) Names the miss ---
3 points: Treated rejection as real in own words; no soft-spin; did not make Claire the whole story.
  Example: "Leadership was right to flag the gaps."

1 point: Engaged with miss Claire already named without minimizing OR named but heavily softened.
  Example: "It shouldn't happen" (engaged, not scapegoating); no restate required for 1.

0 points: Hid, spun, comfort-only, or made Claire entire cause.
  Example: Only "don't worry" with no engagement with the miss.

--- G2 (max 3) Claire owns the leadership conversation (Q&A) ---
3 points: Clear that Claire leads/answers in the leadership conversation AND held when she pushed for shared answering (NO-PUSH if no push). Manager may also be present in the room — presence alone is NOT a fail.
  Examples (3): "You handle the call; I'll be in the room"; "You carry the conversation; I won't answer for you"; "No, you will handle it solo" and held.

1 point: Hedged on who answers, or presence without a clear Claire-owns-Q&A line.
  Example: "Maybe I can jump in if needed"; "I'll be there" with no who-answers line.

0 points: Took leadership Q&A, answered for Claire, or agreed to defend/answer together.
  Examples (0): "I'll handle their questions"; "We'll answer together"; "I'll take the meeting and talk to leadership."
  Do NOT score 0 only because the learner said they will be in the room / present, if Claire still owns answering.

--- G3 (max 4) Check before leadership sees it again ---
4 points: Named check path (draft to manager, review before resubmit) AND Claire presents/answers in leadership conversation.
  Example: "Send me the draft for review; you present to leadership."

2 points: Named only one of those two.
  Example: Only "send me the draft first" without who presents; OR only "you own that meeting" without review gate.

0 points: Left both unanswered.
  Note: timing-only line from expectation_setting G3 does not alone fill this goal.
  Note: "I'll take the leadership meeting" / "I'll answer their questions" fails G2, not full G3.

SECTION 4 - AGGREGATE SCORES, LEVELS, focusSkill
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Competency score = G1 + G2 + G3 (0-10). sumScore = sum of four skills (0-40). maxScore = 40.
percent = round(sumScore / 40 * 100). score = round((sumScore / 40) * 10), clamp 0-10.
headline: nailed_it if percent >= 80; solid if percent >= 50; else try_again.

PERFECT 10 GUARD (skills): A competency score may be 10 ONLY when every goal is at its max AND none of those points came from absence-only credit (goal with points > 0 and empty quote — e.g. Trust G3 no-steamroll). If any goal used absence credit, cap that skill at 9 even if the raw sum is 10.

Level per skill: 9-10 strong | 7-8 adequate | 3-6 developing | 0-2 needs_work | not administered = not_observed

focusSkill: lowest administered score. Tie-break: building_trust, expectation_setting, delegation, accountability.

SECTION 8 - CALIBRATION (accuracy + stable repeats)

Apply AFTER Sections 1–3. These edges resolve common grader drift. Same evidence → same goal points every run.

8A SCORING DISCIPLINE
- Score goals in order: Trust → expectation_setting → delegation → accountability.
- Lock all 12 goal points BEFORE writing summary/notes/youSaid/tryInstead.
- Never raise or lower a goal to balance another skill or to hit a headline band.
- Prefer the listed band points only (no invented mid values like 2.5).
- If between two bands: use Section 2; on trap holds prefer partial over 0; on soft/minimize language prefer the lower band.
- Bare "ok/yeah/sure/fine" AFTER Claire proposes a trap plan (send-up, co-write, shared Q&A / room loop-in) IS evidence for that pressure — score per Section 2. "Silence is not capitulation" applies only when the learner never answered that proposal.

8B HARD EDGES (do not mis-score)
1) Minimize comfort is NOT Trust G2 credit.
   - "don't worry", "it happens", "no big deal" → building_trust G2 = 0.
   - Same lines also cap accountability G1 at 1 (softened / minimized), never 3.
2) Asking why alone does NOT fill Trust G2. G2 needs human acknowledgment of how it landed (frustration/sting/impact), not only cause.
3) Presence in the room is OK. Fail accountability G2 only if learner takes Q&A, answers for Claire, or agrees to defend/answer together.
   - Clear "you'll lead / you handle questions; I'll be there" → G2 = 3 when held.
   - "I'll be there" / "add me" with no who-answers line → G2 = 1.
   - "OK/yeah" after Claire asks to answer together or loop learner into live defense → G2 = 0.
4) Do not zero a whole skill because the closing goodbye was messy if earlier lines earned points.
5) Do not invent timing credit: expectation_setting G3 needs a day/checkpoint ("tomorrow", "Thursday"), not only "this week" / "soon" / "before leadership" without a time.
6) CREDIT ONCE: map each learner substring to one primary goal. Prefer expectation_setting for block / to-me / timing lines; delegation for ownership + distinct support; accountability for miss-naming + who answers leadership. Review support may still earn delegation G3 when Claire keeps the rewrite and the manager only reviews.
7) Strong runs: missing deadline → expectation_setting G3 = 0 (skill often 7, not 10). Do not pad Accountability to compensate.
8) Weak runs: credit real gates and non-takeover help when present. Do not collapse a mixed-weak call to near-zero total.
