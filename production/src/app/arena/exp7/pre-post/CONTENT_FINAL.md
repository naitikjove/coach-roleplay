## PRE — Claire · Your First 1:1 as a New Manager

### Entry meta (`/arena/exp7/pre-post`)

- **Title:** Your First 1:1 as a New Manager  
- **Character:** Claire · Former peer  
- **Purpose (product chrome):** Check your current skills before the videos, then come back after to see the difference.  
- **What you will be assessed on:** the four competencies above  
- **CTA:** Enter 1:1 Conversation → details URL  

### Details (`/arena/exp7/pre-post/session`)

- **Toolbar (next to Back):** microcourse title — Transitioning from Individual Contributor to Manager  
- **Conversation title (entry card / scenario):** Your First 1:1 as a New Manager  

#### What this conversation is about

1. You are a newly promoted manager: weeks ago Claire was your peer; today Claire reports to you.
2. Last week the team submitted a client presentation that Claire owned; yesterday senior leadership rejected it for an incomplete story, thin claims, and numbers that could not hold.
3. Your manager expects a recovery plan from the team this week, and the presentation deadline remains firm.
4. This regular 1:1 is the first talk since the rejection: the old peer habit of late-night rescue is still in the room, Claire is still adjusting to you as manager, and next steps remain open.

#### Who's in this 1:1

**You** · New manager  
New to the role. Running this first 1:1 after the presentation rejection.

**Claire** · Your report · Former peer  
Owned the presentation that came back from leadership. Still getting used to reporting to you.

#### Objectives (learner text only)

1. Keep Claire open after the rejection; avoid pushing them into defense or shutdown.
2. Agree next presentation actions and timing.
3. Keep ownership with Claire; do not redo the draft yourself.
4. Name the failed review; agree how progress will be checked before resubmit.

## POST — Sam · Your 1:1 After Sam’s Report Was Rejected

### Meta

- **Title:** Your 1:1 After Sam’s Report Was Rejected  
- **Character:** Sam · Former peer and work friend  
- **What you will be assessed on:** same four competencies  

### What this conversation is about

You are a newly promoted manager. Sam was your peer and a work friend. Today Sam reports to you.

The client replied on the report Sam owned. They flagged totals that do not match and two sections that contradict each other.

You have a 1:1 with Sam. This is the first since you became manager, and since the client replied.

Your manager wants a recovery plan and the corrected report this week. Next steps are still open.

### Who the conversation is between

**You — Me (new manager)**  
Recently promoted. Sam was your peer and work friend a month ago. Today Sam reports to you. This 1:1 is the first since the client rejected a report with numbers Sam owned.

**Sam — your report (former peer and work friend)**  
Owned the numbers the client flagged. Shaken by the miss, and still getting used to reporting to you.

### Session summary (learner-facing brief)

The client rejected the report Sam sent them. You’re meeting him in a 1:1 as his manager to talk through what happened and what happens next.

### Objectives — 0/4 (learner text only)

1. Find out from Sam what actually happened with the report.
2. Agree what happens with the report between now and the client meeting.
3. Decide who fixes the mistakes in the report.
4. Decide who goes to the client meeting to walk them through the report.
